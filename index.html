Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the current version of the Ludo Global mobile game handles information when you play.

Effective: September 28, 2026 Last updated: September 28, 2026

About this policy

Ludo Global ("Ludo Global", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of the Ludo Global mobile game handles information when you use the app.

Developer/Publisher: dhami.dev
Privacy contact: parasparepsd@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

The current version of Ludo Global does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal or sensitive user data with our servers or third-party advertising or analytics services .

The current app supports offline gameplay, including Local / Pass-and-Play and Play with Computer.

Player names

For local pass-and-play games, players may enter a display name. These names are used only to identify players during gameplay on the device. The current app does not send those names to a remote server.

Game preferences

Ludo Global stores limited settings locally on your device, including whether sound and haptic feedback are enabled. These settings are stored locally so the app can remember your preferences.

Gameplay information

Game state, dice results, pawn positions, match results, and similar gameplay information are processed locally to operate the game. The current version does not upload this gameplay information to our servers.

2. How We Use Information

  • Operate the game.
  • Display player names during local matches.
  • Remember sound and haptic settings.
  • Provide Normal Ludo, Power Ludo, local multiplayer, and computer-opponent gameplay.

We do not use current gameplay information for advertising, profiling, or cross-app tracking.

3. Internet, Accounts, and Online Features

The current release is designed around offline gameplay and does not currently require a Ludo Global account or login.

Online Match, Private Room, and Play with Friends may appear as planned features, but they are not part of the current implemented online service.

If a future version introduces online multiplayer, accounts, cloud storage, analytics, advertising, social features, or other network services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain those data practices.

4. Third-Party Services

The current app does not intentionally send personal information to third-party advertising or analytics platforms.

Ludo Global uses software libraries needed to provide app functionality, including components for displaying graphics, playing audio, and storing local preferences. Device operating systems and app stores may independently process information under their own privacy policies.

5. Data Sharing and Sale

We do not sell personal information.

The current version does not share personal or sensitive user information with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers because it does not collect that information through a remote service.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or valid legal process. Because the current version does not maintain a server-side user database, we generally do not possess server-side personal information about players.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Local player names and game-session information are used for gameplay on the device and are not maintained in a Ludo Global server-side account.

Sound and haptic preferences may remain stored locally until you change them, clear the app's data, or uninstall the app.

Because the current version does not create a user account or store personal data on our servers, there is currently no server-side account data to delete. Users can remove locally stored app data through device settings or by uninstalling the app.

For privacy or deletion questions, contact us using the privacy contact below.

7. Data Security

The current version minimizes privacy risk by keeping gameplay information and preferences on the user's device instead of storing them in a Ludo Global remote database.

Locally stored information is subject to the protections provided by your device and operating system. No method of electronic storage is guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Ludo Global does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the current version of the app.

Because the current app does not maintain user accounts or a remote personal-data database, we do not intentionally collect children's names, contact information, location, or other personal information through a server.

If you believe personal information has been provided to us in a future version of the service, please contact us.

9. Your Privacy Choices

  • Choose player names used during local gameplay.
  • Enable or disable sound.
  • Enable or disable haptic feedback.
  • Clear locally stored app data through device settings.
  • Uninstall Ludo Global to remove locally stored app information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes, including when online, account, analytics, advertising, cloud, or social features are introduced. When we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date on this page.
