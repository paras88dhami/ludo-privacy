Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Ludo Global handles information when you play the game and when advertisements are displayed.

Effective: September 28, 2026 Last updated: October 26, 2026 Android Game · Normal & Power Ludo

About This Privacy Policy

Ludo Global ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile board game published by dhami.dev.

This Privacy Policy describes how information is used in the current Android version of Ludo Global, including offline gameplay, local game settings, and advertising provided through Google AdMob.

Developer / Publisher: dhami.dev
Privacy Contact: parasparepsd@gmail.com

1. Information Used by Ludo Global

Information Used Locally During Gameplay

Ludo Global supports offline gameplay and does not require users to create an account.

  • Player Display Names: Names entered for local matches are used to identify players during gameplay. These names are not uploaded to a Ludo Global game server.
  • Gameplay Information: Dice rolls, token positions, game actions, AI decisions, and match results are processed locally to operate Normal Ludo and Power Ludo.
  • Game Preferences: Sound and vibration settings are saved locally on your device to remember your choices.
  • Rewarded Advertisement Progress: The number of earned ad-free matches is stored locally to apply your reward during gameplay.

The current version does not provide player accounts, cloud save, profile registration, or active online multiplayer services.

Information Processed by Advertising Services

Ludo Global contains advertisements and uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob).

When the advertising SDK operates, Google may automatically collect or receive information including:

  • IP Address: May be used to estimate an approximate geographic location.
  • Device and App Identifiers: Including Android advertising identifiers where available.
  • App and Advertisement Interactions: Including ad impressions, ad clicks, video views, and related interactions.
  • Diagnostic Information: Including SDK performance, reliability, and diagnostic signals.

Google may process this information for advertising, ad measurement, personalization where permitted, fraud prevention, and security.

Advertising-related information may be transmitted to Google and its relevant advertising partners, even though Ludo Global does not operate its own remote player database.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used for the following purposes:

  • To operate Normal Ludo and Power Ludo matches.
  • To support local multiplayer and computer opponents.
  • To display player names during local games.
  • To remember sound and vibration preferences.
  • To record and apply earned ad-free match rewards.
  • To display and measure advertisements using Google AdMob.
  • To prevent invalid advertising activity and support advertising security.
  • To respond to privacy-related inquiries submitted by users.

We do not upload locally entered player names or match histories to our own servers for advertising.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Ludo Global uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements and support the game.

Types of Advertisements

  • Banner Advertisements: Ads that may appear on game menus or other screens.
  • Interstitial Advertisements: Full-screen advertisements that may appear at match completion or transitions.
  • Rewarded Advertisements: Optional advertisements that players may choose to watch to earn one ad-free match.

Rewarded advertisements are optional. Players can continue using the supported game modes without choosing to watch a rewarded advertisement.

Google and its advertising partners may process advertising-related information according to Google's privacy practices and applicable laws.

For more information, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

Although the supported game modes operate offline, an internet connection may be used to request and display advertisements.

4. Accounts, Internet and Online Gameplay

The current version of Ludo Global supports the following gameplay options:

  • Normal Ludo
  • Power Ludo
  • Local / Pass-and-Play for 2–4 players
  • Play with Computer
  • Computer difficulty selection

These supported gameplay modes do not require users to create an account or sign in.

Features such as Online Match, Private Room, and Play with Friends may appear as planned features, but they are not active online multiplayer services in the current release.

Online connectivity may be used for advertisements. Selecting the in-app Privacy Policy link also opens this webpage in your device's browser.

5. Information Sharing and Disclosure

Ludo Global does not operate a game server that receives player-entered display names or local match histories.

We do not sell locally stored gameplay information.

However, because the game integrates Google AdMob, advertising-related information may be collected and shared by Google's advertising SDK, as described in this Privacy Policy.

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we may receive your email address and the information you provide.

We use this information to respond to inquiries and may disclose information when required by law.

6. Data Storage, Retention and Deletion

Information Stored on Your Device

Ludo Global stores certain information locally, including:

  • Sound preferences
  • Vibration preferences
  • Earned ad-free match counts

Users can generally remove this locally stored information by clearing the application's data through Android settings or uninstalling the game.

Advertising Information

Information processed through Google AdMob is subject to Google's own data retention, deletion, and privacy practices.

Visit Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Account Deletion

Ludo Global currently does not create user accounts. Therefore, there is no Ludo Global cloud account to delete.

Users may contact us regarding privacy inquiries or requests to delete information within our control.

7. Data Security

Ludo Global minimizes direct collection of player information by keeping supported gameplay and game preferences on the user's device.

Advertising-related information is processed by third-party services that maintain their own security practices.

While reasonable efforts are made to protect information, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

Ludo Global does not require players to register an account, provide an email address, or enter their real name to play.

Players may choose display names for local games. This gameplay information is processed locally.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process advertising information described in this policy.

Depending on the user's age, region, and the game's advertising configuration, additional privacy protections and restrictions may apply.

Parents and guardians with privacy concerns may contact us using the email address below.

9. Your Privacy Choices and Rights

Users have several options to manage their privacy.

  • Use a nickname instead of your real name during local gameplay.
  • Change sound and vibration preferences in the game.
  • Clear saved application data through Android device settings.
  • Manage or reset your advertising identifier through Android's privacy controls, where available.
  • Review Google's advertising and privacy controls.
  • Contact us regarding privacy questions or applicable data-protection rights.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information.

We will respond to applicable requests involving information within our control. Information held independently by Google may be subject to Google's own privacy request processes.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Ludo Global introduces new functionality or changes how information is processed.

These updates may include online multiplayer, accounts, analytics, cloud storage, or changes to advertising services.

Significant updates will be reflected in the "Last updated" date displayed on this page.
